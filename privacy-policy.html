Privacy Policy for POPS - Can Sorting Game

Last updated: September 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how POPS - Can Sorting Game ("the App," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you download and use our mobile application. We built Pops as a simple, offline-friendly puzzle game, and this policy explains exactly what limited data collection is involved.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

POPS - Can Sorting Game does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information to play. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information directly.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

POPS - Can Sorting Game saves your gameplay progress — including your best completion times for each difficulty level and your daily puzzle streak — directly on your device. This data:

  • Is stored locally only, in a save file on your device
  • Is never transmitted to us or to any third party
  • Is only accessible to the app itself
  • Can be permanently erased at any time using the "Reset Stats" option within the app's settings

If you uninstall the app or use the in-app reset feature, this locally stored data is deleted.

3. Advertising

POPS - Can Sorting Game displays advertisements through Google AdMob to support free access to the game. AdMob may collect and process certain information automatically in order to serve and measure ads, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as advertising ID)
  • Approximate location (derived from IP address)
  • Ad interaction data (such as impressions and clicks)
  • Device and app information (such as device type, operating system, and app version)

This data collection is performed by Google, not by us directly, and is governed by Google's own privacy practices. You can learn more about how Google uses this information here:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services
  • AdMob's data collection practices
Your ad choices: You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (for example, "Opt out of Ads Personalization" in Android Settings, or "Limit Ad Tracking" on iOS).

5. Children's Privacy

POPS - Can Sorting Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete such information.

6. Third-Party Services

POPS - Can Sorting Game uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for displaying advertisements. See Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

7. Data Security

Since POPS - Can Sorting Game does not collect or transmit personal data beyond what is described above, there is no personal data held by us that requires server-side security measures. Locally stored game data remains on your device under your device's own security protections.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
